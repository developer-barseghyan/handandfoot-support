# Support

**Hand and Foot: Canasta Offline** is a relaxed Hand and Foot card game for Android. Play against computer opponents and partners, with your own house rules and cards big enough to read comfortably.

The game works entirely offline. There is no account and no server.

[View on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tigranbarseghyan.handandfoot)

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. The game, the computer players, the tutorial, the rules and your statistics all work without internet.

The app uses the internet for three things only: showing ads, showing the privacy (consent) form where the law requires one, and buying or restoring the "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play. If you are offline you can still play; ads simply will not load.

### How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It is linked to the Google account you used to buy it, so you never need to buy it again on a new phone or after reinstalling.

1. Open the **Play Store** and check that it is signed in with the **same Google account** you used to buy "Remove Ads".
2. Install Hand and Foot from that account.
3. Connect to the internet and open the app. It checks with Google Play and turns "Remove Ads" back on.
4. Still seeing ads? Close the app completely and open it again while online.

You can see the purchase in the Play Store under your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Budget & history**. If it is there and ads still appear, email me the order number (it starts with "GPA."). Refunds are handled by Google Play under [Google Play's refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

### Where is my saved game?

The app saves automatically after every move, on your device. Open the app again and continue exactly where you left off. There is no save button to press.

Your saved game, settings, house rules and statistics are stored only on your device. I have no server and cannot see or recover them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings deletes them.

### How do I change the house rules?

When you start a new game you choose a rule set:

- **Classic (most common)**: the way Hand and Foot is commonly played in the US and Canada. 11-card hand and foot, opening minimums of 50, 90, 120 and 150.
- **Pagat (Whitnack)**: a longer, more strategic game with 13-card hands and feet and wild books.
- **Simple / Beginner**: 50 to open every round, and the pile is easy to take.
- **Your own**: open the house-rules editor, start from any preset, change what you like (hand and foot size, number of decks, round minimums, pile pickup, frozen pile, black threes, wild-card limits, book bonuses, going-out requirements, asking your partner, red threes and more), and save it under your own name.

If a combination of rules cannot work, the app tells you in plain words before you play. The full list of rules is on the rules page inside the app.

### How do I make the cards bigger?

Open **Settings** and choose a **card size**: Normal, Large or Extra Large. In Settings you can also choose a light, dark or high-contrast theme, switch to a left-handed layout, and slow down or speed up the animations.

The app works with Android's TalkBack screen reader, on phones and tablets, in portrait or landscape.

### When do ads appear?

Ads never interrupt your play. A full-screen ad may appear only between rounds or after a game, at most once per round and at least three minutes apart, and never in your first session. There are no banner ads.

You can also choose to watch a short ad to see a "Why?" hint. The one-time **"Remove Ads"** purchase removes the ads between rounds and makes "Why?" hints free.

### How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

Open **Settings** and tap **Privacy options** to review or change the choices you made in the consent form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings (usually **Settings, Privacy, Ads**, or **Settings, Google, Ads**). See the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy).

### Does the computer cheat?

No. The computer players never look at hidden cards: not your hand, not your foot, not the stock. They know only what a person at the table could know, such as their own cards and the cards that have been played or shown. Expert is strong because it remembers and reasons about what it has seen, not because it peeks.

### What does the "Why?" button do?

"Why?" shows what the computer would do in your place, and the reason. It is a good way to learn. Each hint is unlocked by watching a short optional ad, or is free once you own "Remove Ads".

### I'm new to Hand and Foot. Where do I start?

Try the **interactive tutorial** in the app, then play a game with the **Simple / Beginner** rules and the computer on **Easy**. The rules page in the app explains everything in plain words.

### The computer played something that seems wrong.

Please tell me. Send the rule set you were using, what the computer did, and what you expected instead. A screenshot of the table at that moment is ideal. Every report like this improves the computer players for everyone.
