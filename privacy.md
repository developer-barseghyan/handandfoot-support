# Privacy Policy

**Hand and Foot: Canasta Offline**

Effective date: 20 October 2026  
Developer: Tigran Barseghyan  
Contact: **developer.barseghyan@gmail.com**

This policy covers the Android app with package name `com.tigranbarseghyan.handandfoot`.

---

## In short

I do not collect your personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no developer server and no analytics in the app. Your games, settings, house rules and statistics stay on your device.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google, as an independent third party, collects some device information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings, Privacy options**.

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. I never see your payment details.

---

## What is stored on your device

- the game in progress, saved after every move so you can resume it
- your settings, such as card size, theme, layout and animation speed
- your custom house-rule sets
- your statistics: games played and won, best score, books made, clean book rate

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else, and I cannot access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings deletes it. Depending on your device settings, Android's own backup service may include app data in your Google account backup; that is controlled by you and by Google, not by me.

## What I collect

**Nothing.** I run no server for this app, and the app contains no analytics or tracking code of my own. I do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

If you email me for support, I receive your email address and what you write. I use it only to reply and delete it when it is no longer needed.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you buy "Remove Ads", the app shows full-screen ads between rounds or after a game, and offers optional reward ads that unlock a "Why?" hint. These ads are provided by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). Google acts as an independent third party.

When ads are requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app sends Google information such as:

- device identifiers, including your Android **advertising ID** and app set ID
- your **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
- app interactions, such as app launches and ad views
- diagnostic information, such as launch time and crash or hang rate

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. I do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; AdMob gives me only aggregate reports, such as how many ads were shown.

See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings (usually **Settings, Privacy, Ads**, or **Settings, Google, Ads**).

## Your consent and how to change it

The app uses Google's **User Messaging Platform** to show a consent or privacy message where the law requires one, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and US states with consumer privacy laws. Where required, the message appears **before any ad loads**, and ads follow the choices you make. To decide whether a message is needed and to remember your choice, Google processes basic device information such as your IP address; your choice is stored on your device.

To change your choices at any time, open the app and go to **Settings, Privacy options**.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play** under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Play Terms of Service. I never receive your payment card or bank details. Google Play tells the app whether you own "Remove Ads" so it can turn ads off. Google also gives me limited transaction records (order number, product, price, date, and the country of sale), which I use only for accounting, tax, refunds and support.

## When the app uses the internet

The game works offline. The app connects to the internet only to request and show ads, to show the consent message, and to make or restore purchases through Google Play. Links in the app open in your web browser.

## Children

The app is designed for adults and is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age in your country. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children; the app itself collects none from anyone. If you believe a child has used the app and want to discuss the advertising data described above, please contact me.

## Your rights

Because I do not collect or keep personal information about you, there is usually nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Your game data is on your device, and you control it. For advertising data, use **Settings, Privacy options** in the app, your Android advertising ID controls, and the tools Google provides at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and [your Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/). You may also contact me about any request, and I will help as far as I can.

**European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland.** If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time (in the app, under **Settings, Privacy options**) without affecting earlier processing. Personalized advertising is based on your consent. Google processes advertising data as an independent controller under its own policies and may transfer data outside your country with appropriate safeguards. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

**United States.** Residents of California and other states with consumer privacy laws have rights to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising. I do not sell personal information and do not collect it myself. When the app shows personalized ads through Google AdMob, Google collects the information listed under "Advertising" above, which under some state laws may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". To opt out, use **Settings, Privacy options** in the app, or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android Settings. No sensitive personal information is collected. I will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

## Security and retention

I keep no personal information on any server. Data collected by Google's advertising and Play services is encrypted in transit and kept by Google according to Google's policies. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it and the updated version appears at this address. If a change is significant, for example if the app starts collecting new kinds of data, it will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
